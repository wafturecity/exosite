Any clickable listings means I've farmed the song for pp before (I'll limit this to my top 100)and is linked to my score (hardest score on the song, unless mentioned otherwise)

pp, AKA Performance Points, is a metric in osu! of (usually) how hard a map is, and the harder the map, the more rewarding it is. Since no system is perfect, there are some note patterns which are overweight (give more pp than they should actually be worth), and are hence dubbed "farm patterns" or "slop patterns". Farming is the act of gaining pp.

because i like long songs/maps :3
(sure sometimes i might play a short map to focus on a specific skillset, for example this map for spaced flow aim precision, 1:30 hp drain time)

spaced: the note is far away from the last one
flow aim: instead of snap aiming, where you snap to the notes, mainly used for jumps, flow aim requires you to follow a pattern with smooth aim, flowing like a river
precision: small notes, meaning you need your aim to be more precise
so you need to aim smoothly onto notes that are far away from eachother, while being precise with your aim

hp drain time is how much gameplay time a map has, and yes its different from map length (hp drain time doesn't include breaks, nor the start of a song that doesnt have notes, or the end of a song that doesnt have notes)
that's why the 3 "Save Me"s have different hp drain times.
